Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Applies to cybercontrol.fun, app.cybercontrol.fun, api.cybercontrol.fun, and the CyberControl AutoFill Chrome extension.

CyberControl is software for Indian cybercafé operators. It stores customer profiles, documents, and photos in a café workspace, and can fill government and portal forms from those profiles when the operator asks it to.

The café operator (the workspace owner) decides which customer data is stored. CyberControl hosts that workspace. We do not sell customer data. We do not use it for advertising or credit scoring.

1. Who this covers

  • Café operators and staff who sign in to Hub.
  • Customers whose details an operator stores to complete a service (forms, documents, photos).
  • People who contact us from the public website.

2. Data we handle

Operator account. Sign-in identifiers (such as email or phone), workspace membership, session tokens, and product settings (theme, fill preferences).

Customer profiles the operator saves. Name, parent/spouse name, date of birth, gender, address, identity numbers (including Aadhaar when the operator enters them), contact numbers, uploaded documents, photos, and signatures.

WhatsApp intake. Files and messages a customer sends to the café’s connected WhatsApp number, filed into a profile by the operator.

Form fill (Chrome extension). After the operator chooses a customer and taps Fill this form, the extension reads visible field labels on the current page, loads the selected profile from Hub, and types matching values. It records fill results (filled / skipped / failed) for that workspace. It does not submit the form.

Technical data. IP address, timestamps, error logs, and mapping quality needed to run the product.

3. What we do not collect

  • Passwords, OTP, CAPTCHA, or payment-card values from form pages.
  • A record of every website the operator visits. The extension’s Hub bridge runs only on CyberControl Hub. Fill scripts run on a form page only after the operator taps Fill this form.
  • Browsing history for advertising.

4. Chrome extension

CyberControl AutoFill is a Manifest V3 extension. All executable code ships in the extension package. The API returns profile values and field mappings (data), not scripts to execute from the open web.

The extension stores a Hub session token, API URL, theme, and last selected customer in Chrome storage so the side panel stays signed in. It talks to https://api.cybercontrol.fun and https://app.cybercontrol.fun over HTTPS / WSS.

Host access to http/https pages is used only to fill the form tab the operator is working on. File pages are not accessed.

5. Why we use the data

  • To run Hub: remember customers, files, photos, and documents for the café.
  • To fill the current form when the operator taps Fill this form.
  • To improve field mapping inside that café workspace.
  • To provide optional AI / MCP tools only after the operator grants that access in Hub or the side panel.
  • To keep the service signed in, secure, and working.

6. Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate CyberControl:

  • Hosting and database providers that run our servers (including AWS).
  • The public website host (Vercel) for cybercontrol.fun.
  • WhatsApp, when the café connects a number to receive customer files.
  • AI or MCP providers, only when an operator turns on that feature and approves the specific access.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Workspace data stays until the operator deletes the customer, document, or account, or the workspace is closed. Operators can edit or delete customer profiles in Hub. To delete an operator account or a whole workspace, use Contact on cybercontrol.fun.

8. Security

Access to Hub and the API requires a signed-in operator. Data in transit uses HTTPS. Sensitive form fields (OTP, password, payment, CAPTCHA) are skipped by fill and are not stored from the page.

9. Children’s data

CyberControl is built for café operators, not for children. Customer profiles may include a minor’s details when an adult is completing a government service for them. That data is stored because the operator entered it for that service.

10. Operator responsibility

Operators should collect and store only what they need for the service the customer asked for, and should have the customer’s permission to keep those documents in the café workspace.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: use Contact on cybercontrol.fun, or sign in to Hub and open Settings.
